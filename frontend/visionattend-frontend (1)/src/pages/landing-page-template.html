FAQ

Questions, answered.

A few things worth knowing before you put a camera in the attendance workflow.

What makes VisionAttend different from manual attendance?
VisionAttend is designed around an actual camera-based attendance workflow: recognize the student, verify liveness, connect the result to a session and reflect it in the dashboard.
Can students fake attendance with a photo?
The system includes liveness detection before recognition. It is designed to make basic photo or video spoofing harder, while deployments should still be tested and hardened for their own environment.
Who can use the platform?
Admins manage students, sessions, attendance, schedules and leave. Students get their own dashboard for attendance, calendar, leave and account settings.
Is my account protected?
VisionAttend includes bot protection, email verification, password recovery, two-factor authentication and security settings. Keep your deployment secrets private and follow the project's security guidance.
